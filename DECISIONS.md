# Frozen comparison design
Recorded before the corrected experiments. The archived study used a different schedule, channel and workload. Its outputs are preserved in the original ZIP. This revision is a separate study. The JSON syntax in configs/default.yaml is valid YAML 1.2.

- The deployment, rather than the node, is the independent sampling unit. All seed numbers start at zero. Capture the configuration SHA-256 with each CSV. Full settings are 50 static pairs per stratum, 50 loss trials, six seven-day lifetime pairs and three seven-day sensitivity pairs. Small lifetime samples limit generalization. No outcome gate or early stopping by significance.
- E1 measures election and charged association on geometric networks across size and a 400-node grid. E2 uses all eight graph families. E3 uses native LEACH and four controls matched to P5, across degree and size. E4 compares the published Afek schedule (M=1,2,4 and separate oracle bound), published local feedback, EcoBeep and its bias-off twin at both starting-energy profiles, d=6,8. E5 uses all six IDs plus the published local baseline and bias-off twin. E6 crosses loss and symmetry with confirmation on/off and Afek. E7 measures maintenance, floors, idle/receive current and half-duplex sensitivity. The fixed seed-zero E2 illustrations are chosen before inspection.
- P0 deploys the first floor(n/5) IDs as heads for life. P3 sorts living vertices by original live degree, then ID. P1/P2 use the integer epoch. P4 uses inclusive repeats; the observed last decision, schedule exhaustion, and worst-case schedule are distinct. All static election trials disable maintenance.
- EcoBeep confirmation means provisional winners beep together again, withdraw on local detection in that exchange, then surviving candidates announce. Only a received final announcement creates a local coverage declaration. This explicit three-exchange design resolves an incomplete transition in the pasted brief. Half-duplex emulates only exchanges requiring sender feedback. Silent slots always cost scheduled listeners.
- A frame occupies one 40 ms slot, TX airtime from the configured bytes, RX for a full scheduled slot. Heads advertise by ascending ID, all living nonheads listen. Each reachable volunteer requests its nearest advertising head; each head broadcasts the list of accepted IDs. Failed attempts still consume energy. No retry until new association. Speakers can die mid-slot; a packet requires both parties to survive its airtime.
- On each 300 s boundary living nodes re-elect (fixed heads retain their roles). Every living source generates one report at 60,120,... seconds strictly before the administrative horizon. Setup takes priority; reports generated during a busy operation are dropped. Each upload and aggregate has its own MAC slot. A data batch expires at the next report time. Sink links cost the usual TX energy and require no member-to-sink routing. Sink energy is outside the network budget.
- Maintenance ticks follow wall time. At a tie, finish in-flight frames and account for deaths, then clustering, maintenance, traffic. A busy radio defers maintenance to the next available tick; heartbeat opportunities occur every 50 ticks. Weak heads demote once per eligible tick, cool down for 100 ticks and may join another head. Three missed heartbeats start local repair. Existing heads defend their neighborhood in repair contention/confirmation and announce. Unresolved orphans retry at later heartbeat opportunities. Reassociate after role changes.
- Primary E4 measure is median paired physical-slot ratio P4/P5. Failures score the common 4096-slot cap; give raw times, differences, successes and successful-pair denominator. Paired percentile bootstrap, 2000 resamples. Exact two-sided Clopper-Pearson violation intervals. E5 endpoint medians use Kaplan-Meier with administrative censoring, including unobserved endpoints; fixed-denominator service counts every initial source opportunity until the horizon even after all deaths. Full versus bias-off changes only the bias switch.
- Idle debit applies continuously including long quiet intervals. Deaths are timed inside a mode segment, budgets stop at zero. CPU/aggregation and additional sleep energy are zero. Receipt is Boolean in elections. Sender IDs are observer-only except in the separately modeled addressed packets.
- Named SHA-256 keys use UTF-8 JSON arrays. Node-level draws use a keyed 32-bit integer mixer by opportunity, node and purpose; directed losses key absolute slot and endpoints, sorted for symmetric loss. Graph adjacency is indexed by receiver. Output uses UTF-8, LF and deterministic iteration. Smoke uses shorter horizons and separately labelled accelerated batteries. Numerical results are read from experiment CSVs.
